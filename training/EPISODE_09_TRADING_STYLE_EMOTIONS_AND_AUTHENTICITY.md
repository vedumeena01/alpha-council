## 1. Technical & Execution Mechanics

### A. The Three Entry Types

```
  Entry Type 1: Set & Forget (Limit order just above Proximal Line; standard execution)
  Entry Type 2: Confirmation Entry (Wait for candle to close in zone, enter on next candle open)
  Entry Type 3: Breakout Confirmation (Wait for 1st candle to close inside, 2nd candle to leave zone)
```

1. **Entry Type 1: Set and Forget (The Institutional Gold Standard):**
   - **Mechanics:** Limit order placed slightly above the Proximal Line ($PL + \text{buffer}$) with stop-loss below the Distal Line ($DL - \text{buffer}$).
   - **Application:** Used on pristine $7/7$ setups and high-conviction coinciding setups.
   - **Advantage:** Zero screen-watching required during market hours. Institutional limit orders execute automatically.

2. **Entry Type 2: In-Zone Close & Next-Bar Open:**
   - **Purpose:** Developed for slightly compromised zones (e.g. $6/7$ or $5/7$ with $4-5$ base candles).
   - **Rule 1:** Incoming price candle must enter and close **inside the zone** ($DL \le \text{Close} \le PL$).
   - **Rule 2:** Trade is executed **only when the second candle opens inside the zone** ($DL \le \text{Open} \le PL$).
   - **Key Scenarios:**
     - *Gap Down below DL:* Entry is aborted, saving the trader from an immediate stop-loss hit!
     - *Gap Up above PL:* Entry is missed; price departs without filling.

3. **Entry Type 3: Zone Departure Confirmation:**
   - **Rule:** First candle closes inside zone; wait for a subsequent candle to physically exit the zone on the upside before buying.
   - **The Flaw:** By waiting for departure, the entry price is pushed significantly higher, inflating risk and drastically shrinking the Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR).

4. **The Instructor's Verdict:**
   - Arun Singh Tanwar personally trades **Entry Type 1 exclusively**.
   - Type 2 & Type 3 require staring at screens from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM. A trader's time is far better spent scanning for clean $7/7$ setups traded with Set & Forget.

## 2. Authenticity & Credibility of Zones

Not all fresh zones carry equal institutional weight:

```
  Authentic Zone:     Forms independently in virgin space (origin of institutional order flow).
  Non-Authentic Zone: Forms as an immediate reaction to a preceding historical zone.
```

1. **Authentic Zone:** A demand or supply zone originating in clean price territory without bouncing off an earlier underlying zone.
2. **Non-Authentic Zone:** When price retraces into an established Demand Zone 1, produces a bounce, and in doing so forms a new Demand Zone 2, Zone 2 is **Non-Authentic** because its momentum was borrowed from Zone 1's pending orders.
3. **Tradeability Rules for Non-Authentic Zones:**
   - **Non-Tradeable by Default:** If the leg-out fails to close above the prior leg-in / overhead resistance.
   - **Tradeable Exception:** If the leg-out delivers an explosive, wide-range departure that closes decisively above the prior leg-in and overhead supply (`has_clean_closing == True` & `is_proven_zone == True`).
4. **The Retest Vulnerability (Level on Top of Level - LOTL):**
   - When price returns to retest a non-authentic zone, it frequently pierces through it to seek the original, authentic base (Zone 1).
   - In LOTL stacked scenarios, conservative traders place orders on the lower authentic base (Zone 1).

## 3. Trading Styles Evaluation: Intraday vs. Positional

| Trading Style | Higher Timeframe (Location) | Intermediate Timeframe (Trend) | Lower Timeframe (Execution) | Nature of Style & Holding Period |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HIT (Hourly Income)** | 60m / 75m | 15m | 5m | Ultra-quick intraday scalps (minutes to hours) |
| **DIT (Daily Income)** | Daily | 60m / 75m | 15m | Standard intraday (closed by 3:15 PM) |
| **WIT (Weekly Income)** | Weekly | Daily | 75m / 125m | Short-term swing (2 to 7+ days) |
| **MIT (Monthly Income)** | Monthly | Weekly | Daily | Positional delivery (weeks to months) |

### A. Pitfalls of Intraday Trading (HIT / DIT):
1. **Micro Stop-Loss Vulnerability:** Stop-losses are narrow (₹2 to ₹6). A single 500-1,000 share retail block trade, news spike, or normal Nifty 30-40 pt swing can trigger stop-losses instantly.
2. **Slippage, Commission & Tax Drag:** High turnover results in heavy STT, GST, brokerage fees, and execution slippage.
3. **Screen Burnout:** Continuous 6-hour monitoring leads to emotional fatigue and impulsive revenge-trading.

### B. The Superiority of Positional Styles (WIT / MIT):
1. **Structural Durability:** Stop-losses are backed by multi-day institutional volume, immune to minor intraday noise.
2. **The Rupee-Risk Equality Rule:**
   $$\text{Quantity} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Total Risk (INR)}}{\text{Stop-Loss Distance (Points)}} \right\rfloor$$
   A ₹50 stop-loss in an MIT setup and a ₹2 stop-loss in a DIT setup carry the **exact same ₹1,000 monetary risk**!
3. **The Mandatory 100-Trade Directive:** Every student must execute a minimum of **100 trades strictly in WIT and MIT** before attempting active intraday trading (DIT/HIT).

## 4. Emotional Mastery: The Rejection Mindset

```
                       EMOTIONS & TRADING PSYCHOLOGY
 ┌──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
 │ The Rejection Mindset│ Intelligence Paradox │   The Luck Myth      │
 │  (Filter for Quality)│ (Mindset > Degrees)  │(Probabilities Rule)  │
 └──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Rejection Mindset:**
   - Novice traders hunt for reasons to click "Buy" (forcing distorted zones and diagonal lines).
   - Professional traders actively search for reasons to **REJECT** the trade:
     - Is the ITF trend against the trade? $\rightarrow$ **Reject.**
     - Did the leg-out fail clean closing? $\rightarrow$ **Reject.**
     - Is the execution zone far from Shri Krishna without $7/7$ score? $\rightarrow$ **Reject.**
     - Is it a non-authentic reaction hitting immediate overhead resistance? $\rightarrow$ **Reject.**
     - Does the opposing ITF target offer $< 2:1$ RRR? $\rightarrow$ **Reject.**
     - Does the LTF distal line breach HTF distal line (Boundary Rule)? $\rightarrow$ **Reject.**
   - Setups that survive aggressive rejection possess pristine institutional quality. This eliminates over $50\%$ of trading anxiety automatically.

2. **The Intelligence Paradox & The Luck Myth:**
   - Academic degrees (IIT, MBA, CA, MD) do not correlate with market profitability. Markets reward emotional discipline, not raw IQ.
   - Professional trading has zero luck; only traders aligned with macro institutional order flow capture consistent moves.
